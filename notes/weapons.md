# Weapons

Weapons with higher speed fire first during the combat turn.  
Dominator weapons can have a slightly lower minimum weight.

### Treton

Can shoot asteroids without destroying the minerals in them.  
Each shot drains life proportional to the damage dealt. Exact ratio unknown.  
Each shot slows down the enemy by a percentage equal to the damage dealt.  
Treton alone can not slow a ship below 50% of its base speed.
